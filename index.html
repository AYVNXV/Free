Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Is WebToolVault really free?

Yes. All tools are free to use, with no accounts, no ads and no payment required.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Your files are processed locally in your browser and are never uploaded to our servers. This is the main idea behind WebToolVault.

Why does the video converter download an engine first?

The video converter uses FFmpeg, which runs inside your browser. The first time you use it, your browser downloads the engine (about 30 MB) from a public CDN. After that it is usually cached by your browser, and the conversion itself happens on your device.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no sign-up or login anywhere on the website.

Is there a file size limit?

We do not set a limit. Because your device does the work, very large files can be slow or fail if your browser runs out of memory. For big videos, a desktop browser works best.

Which browsers are supported?

Modern versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave and Safari work well. Keeping your browser updated gives the best results.

Will the quality of my file change?

It depends on the format. Lossy formats (like JPG, MP3 or MP4) and image compression trade a little quality for a smaller file. Keep your original file until you have checked the result.
