1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how WebToolVault ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use our website and online tools, including our image, audio, video, document, compressor and resizer tools (together, the "Service").

Our approach is simple: we built WebToolVault so that we never need access to your files or personal data. By using the Service you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

2. How WebToolVault Works

All tools on WebToolVault run client-side, which means the work happens inside your web browser, on your own computer, phone or tablet, using technologies such as JavaScript, WebAssembly and the browser's built-in APIs.

  • When you select or drag a file into a tool, it is read directly by your browser.
  • The conversion, compression, resizing or editing is performed on your device.
  • The result is created in your browser's memory and offered to you as a download.
  • Your original file and the result are never transmitted to our servers.
  • When you close or refresh the page, temporary data in browser memory is released.
Because processing happens on your device, the speed of a tool depends on your device's power and memory.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, receive, store or have access to any of the following:

  • Your files (images, videos, audio, documents, text, or any other content) or their contents.
  • File names, file sizes, file types or metadata of files you process.
  • Your name, email address, phone number or postal address (we have no accounts or sign-up forms).
  • Payment information (the Service is completely free).
  • Your precise location.
  • Advertising profiles or behavioural data. We do not run ads.

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your personal information, because we do not collect it.

4. Technical Data and Server Logs

Like almost every website, when you visit WebToolVault your browser requests our pages from our web hosting provider. Standard web servers may automatically record basic technical information about that request, for example:

  • IP address
  • Date and time of the request
  • Page or file requested
  • Browser type and operating system (user agent)
  • Referring page

This information is generated by the normal operation of web hosting. We use it only to keep the website running, secure and stable (for example, to detect abuse or fix errors). We do not use it to identify you, and we do not combine it with any files you process, because we never receive those files.

5. Third-Party Services (CDN)

To keep our website fast and to avoid loading heavy files from our own server, some tools download open-source libraries from public Content Delivery Networks (CDNs). For example, the Video Converter downloads the FFmpeg engine (about 25–30 MB) from a public CDN the first time you enable it.

Service Purpose What they may see
jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) Delivers open-source libraries such as FFmpeg Your IP address and standard request details when the library is downloaded
unpkg (unpkg.com) Backup CDN for the same libraries Your IP address and standard request details when the library is downloaded
Google Favicon Service (google.com/s2/favicons) Shows website logos on our Free Resources / Storage directory pages Your IP address and standard request details when the logos load

These CDNs only deliver the library files to your browser. They never receive your files, because your files are never sent anywhere. These providers have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

Our resource directory pages (such as Free Storage and Tools Directory) show logos of other websites using Google's favicon service. This only loads small logo images. No files you process are ever involved.

We do not use advertising networks, social media tracking pixels, or third-party behavioural analytics on WebToolVault.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

WebToolVault does not use cookies to track you, and we do not use advertising or profiling cookies.

Your browser may cache the downloaded library files (such as the video engine) so that the next visit loads faster. This cache is stored on your own device and is managed by your browser. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

If a tool stores a simple preference on your device (for example, a selected option), it is saved only in your browser and is never sent to us.

7. Security

The strongest protection for your data is that it never leaves your device. Since your files are not uploaded, they cannot be intercepted on their way to us, leaked from our servers, or exposed in a data breach of our systems.

We take reasonable steps to keep the website secure, including serving it over HTTPS. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure. You are also responsible for keeping your own device, browser and operating system up to date and free of malware.

8. Data Retention

  • Your files: We never receive them, so we never retain them. Temporary data in your browser's memory is cleared when you close or refresh the page.
  • Server logs: Basic hosting logs, if any, are kept only for a limited period needed for security and maintenance, according to our hosting provider's settings.
  • Messages you send us: If you email us, we keep the message only as long as needed to respond and handle your request.

9. Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect personal data and do not offer accounts, there is no child data to store. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
